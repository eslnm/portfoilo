About Me

I am an Aerospace Engineering student at Zewail City of Science and Technology, one of Egypt's most prestigious research universities. My academic journey sits at the intersection of rigorous theoretical physics, applied mathematics, and practical engineering design.

My passion lies in transforming abstract aerospace concepts into working engineering solutions. Whether I am modeling the thermodynamic cycle of a steam power plant, designing a closed-loop pitch autopilot, or simulating rocket guidance trajectories, I approach every problem with the precision and curiosity that real aerospace engineering demands.

I work extensively with MATLAB, Simulink, Simscape, and SolidWorks — the same toolchain used by aerospace engineers at major institutions worldwide. My project experience spans propulsion systems, flight control, structural vibration analysis, and computational thermodynamics.

My long-term ambition is to contribute to next-generation aircraft, spacecraft, and propulsion systems — pushing the frontiers of what is aerodynamically and thermodynamically possible. I am actively seeking research internships and opportunities that will accelerate this mission.

Aircraft Longitudinal Pitch Autopilot
Developed a closed-loop aircraft pitch control system using Root Locus techniques. Designed and validated PID controllers in MATLAB and Simulink while satisfying transient performance requirements and stability criteria including rise time, overshoot, and settling time specifications.

PROJECT_03

650 MW Regenerative Reheat Rankine Cycle
Built a comprehensive MATLAB simulation for a 650 MW regenerative steam power plant. Evaluated multiple feedwater heater configurations (open & closed), optimized extraction pressures using parametric sweeps, and improved thermal efficiency through advanced cycle analysis.
MATLAB XSteam Thermodynamics Optimization

Cantilever Beam Vibration Analysis
Conducted experimental modal testing to identify natural frequencies and damping characteristics of a cantilever beam. Compared analytical predictions with experimental measurements using advanced vibration analysis techniques, validating theoretical models against physical reality.

Rocket Guidance & Trajectory Simulation
Developed a nonlinear MATLAB simulation framework for rocket interception scenarios. Implemented Runge-Kutta integration, aerodynamic drag models, and Proportional Navigation guidance laws to analyze trajectory performance and miss distance under realistic flight conditions.
